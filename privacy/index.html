Bossaroo

Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

  • You can use Bossaroo without giving us your name or your email.
  • We save your progress so you don't lose it.
  • What you type in the Practice room is sent to an AI to write the replies, hints and your feedback. We don't keep it on our servers. Your last 7 chats are saved on your phone only, and you can delete them there.
  • We count a few things you do in the app, such as finishing a lesson, to see where people stop. You can turn that off in Settings.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.
  • You can delete your account, and everything saved with it, from Settings.

Who we are

Bossaroo is made by James, in Canada. If you have a question about your data, email privacy@bossaroo.com.

What we collect, and why

An anonymous account. The first time you open Bossaroo, it makes an account for you with a random ID. It has no name or email. We use it to save your progress, and to give everyone a fair daily share of AI practice.

Your progress. Which lessons you've finished, your checkpoint results, your Daily Challenge answers, the days you played (for your streak), and the skill tree you're on. It's saved on your phone and in our database, so it survives a reinstall.

Your email, only if you sign in. We use it to send you a one-time sign-in code, and to bring your progress to a new phone. We don't send marketing emails.

Daily Challenge picks. To show what share of managers picked each answer, each pick is saved with a random ID made by the app on your phone. That ID isn't linked to your account, your email or your name.

How much AI you used. For each account, we count how many AI replies and reviews it used each day, so we can keep to a daily limit. In the Practice room, each reply, hint or set of suggested replies counts as one reply, and so does asking how a message landed, and the review at the end counts as one review. We also add up what the account's AI cost us each month, as one amount, so we can keep to a monthly limit. We store the counts and the amount only, with nothing you wrote.

Your Practice room chats. In the Practice room, you describe a conversation you're worried about and practice it with an AI that plays the other person. What you type there goes through our server to Anthropic's Claude: the setup (the person's name and role, what you wrote about the situation, and the three sliders) and every message you send. Claude uses it only to write the other person's replies, the coach's hints, suggested replies and feedback on a message, and your review at the end. We don't store or log what you type on our servers. Our server's records show how much each request cost and how many replies each account used that day, never what anyone wrote. Please use a made-up name for the other person, and leave out anything private, so nothing you type points to a real person.

Your past chats, on your phone only. So you can read a chat again or pick it back up, the app saves your last 7 Practice room chats on your phone: the setup, what you and the AI wrote, the coach's hints and feedback, and the review. A chat is saved from the first message you send, and an eighth chat replaces the oldest. They never go to our servers, and they aren't saved with your account, so they don't follow you to a new phone when you sign in. You can delete one chat, or all of them, on the Practice tab. Signing out, deleting your account or uninstalling the app removes them from your phone too.

If you've turned on Android's backup, Google may include the app's data on your phone, and these chats with it, in the backup in your own Google account, and put it back when you reinstall the app. We never see that copy. Delete your past chats on the Practice tab first if you don't want them in a backup.

Anthropic processes the chat under its commercial terms: it doesn't use it to train its models, and it deletes it within 30 days, unless a chat is flagged for breaking Anthropic's usage rules.

The Practice room can help with what to say, but it can't give legal or HR advice. For a conversation like letting someone go, check with HR first.

What stays on your phone. Your answers in the first few onboarding screens, your Manager Snapshot, your reminder time and your last 7 Practice room chats are saved on your phone and not in our database. What you type in a chat does go through our server to the AI while you're chatting, as described above, but we don't keep it. Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone too: we don't send push notifications from a server.

Technical logs. The services we use keep standard records, such as IP addresses and the times of requests, to keep things secure and running.

Crash reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, a report goes to Sentry, our error-reporting service, with your device model, your OS version, the app version and what went wrong in the code. It never includes anything from a Practice room chat or anything you typed, and we don't keep your IP address with it.

Usage counts. We count a fixed list of things you do in the app, to see which parts work and where people give up. The list: opening the app, reaching each onboarding screen, signing up or skipping it, starting or finishing a lesson, starting a roleplay chat, how a chat or checkpoint ended (passed, not yet or skipped), playing the daily scene, seeing the plan screen, and tapping a reminder. Each count can carry one short label: which lesson, chat, scene or onboarding screen it was, or which part of the app you opened the plan screen from. The app version and the time are saved with it. Each count is saved with your account's ID, so we can see where people stop. The count has no name or email in it, but if you've signed in, your account does have your email. It never includes anything you type, your answers or picks, or your chat messages. The Practice room adds nothing to this list. The counts stay in our own database at Supabase, and no analytics company gets them. To turn this off, go to Settings → Share usage counts. It's on unless you turn it off. Turning it off stops new counts, and drops any still waiting on your phone. The ones already in our database stay until they're 12 months old or you delete your account.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We don't use outside analytics or tracking services. The usage counts above stay in our own database.
  • We don't use your chats to train AI.

How long we keep it

Your account, progress, AI counts and monthly AI amounts stay until you delete your account. Usage counts are deleted after 12 months, or sooner if you delete your account. Daily Challenge picks aren't linked to you, so they stay, as part of the anonymous totals. Your past Practice room chats are only on your phone: it keeps the last 7 until you delete them, sign out, delete your account or uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my account. That deletes your account, your progress, your AI counts and your monthly AI amounts from our database straight away, and clears them from your phone, along with your past Practice room chats. Your usage counts are deleted from our database at the same time. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Uninstalling the app isn't the same as deleting your account. If you haven't signed in, your progress becomes impossible to reach, but it stays in our database. So do your usage counts, until they're 12 months old. Delete your account first if you want them gone. Your past Practice room chats were only on your phone, so uninstalling removes them, unless Android's backup has a copy (see "Your past chats" above).

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to delete it, by emailing privacy@bossaroo.com. A copy includes your usage counts. You can also ask us to correct your email address. Usage counts only record what happened in the app, so there's nothing in them to correct, but we'll delete them if you ask. We'll answer within 30 days. If you're not happy with how we handled it, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Bossaroo is for people who manage others at work. It isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Keeping it safe

Everything between the app and our server is encrypted. In our database, each account can reach only its own progress. Our AI keys stay on our server and never ship in the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change it, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
